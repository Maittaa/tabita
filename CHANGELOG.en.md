# Changelog

[Русская версия](CHANGELOG.md)


## 5.70

- Colourful tiles in the Material You spirit: sleep, steps, heart rate, weight, water, food, workout, finances and other “Today” tiles and “Health” sections each get their own colour. The hues are harmonised with your accent colour and wallpaper, as in Google apps, and text on the tiles stays high‑contrast
- Large tiles get a soft tint of their category; switch everything back to the calm look with “Colourful tiles” in “Appearance”

## 5.69

- The timer sound is now chosen from Android’s own sounds: “Personalisation” → “Timer sound” → “Android sound” opens the system picker for notification and alarm sounds, with a play button next to it. The synthesised bells from 5.68 are removed; if you chose one, you are back on “Simple beep”
- The chosen sound plays as an alarm and for no longer than 6 seconds, so a long tune does not get in the way of the next set

## 5.68

- Timer sound in “Personalisation”: five bells — “Hand bell”, “Singing bowl”, “Wind chimes”, “Ding-dong”, “Glockenspiel” — plus “Simple beep” as before. Tap to choose and hear it right away
- The bell rings when rest between sets ends, when a timed strength exercise ends, and at every cardio and yoga block; it plays as an alarm, so you hear it even with media sound off

## 5.67

- Gentle return: if the streak broke, “Today” greets you without blame — how long the break was, that occasional misses barely affect habit forming, and that your freezes are kept
- Soft streak “last 7 days: N of 7” — shown while the in-a-row streak is under two, so progress stays visible even after a break
- Milestones at 14, 30 and 66 filled days: a one-time card explains what the data now allows (“What affects it” findings and comparison, refining calorie burn by weight, reviewing day marks) and takes you there
- English interface: weekdays in “Programme”, the “Full” tile label, the item order hint, the alarm label and “Resting heart rate” in experiments are no longer left in Russian

## 5.66

- “Today”: the progress line is no longer pinned at the top — it scrolls with the tiles, leaving more room to view
- Streak freezes instead of “one forgiven miss per week”: one for every 7 filled days, up to 3 in stock. A missed day uses them automatically; if the gap is longer than your stock, the streak restarts but the freezes are kept. Tap the streak line to see how many you have, how many days until the next one and which days were frozen
- Durations in hints (“6 h 34 min”) no longer break across lines

## 5.65

- “Today”: one or two lines on what today’s data means — sleep, resting heart rate and steps are compared with your own usual level (median of past weeks), shown only when the difference is noticeable; nothing already said by “Daily tips” is repeated
- Also there, a new reliable finding from “What affects it” shows for one day — no need to open the monthly summary to see it
- Sunday week review as a notification at 19:00: sleep, active minutes, weight and protein, one fix for next week and the top finding; can be turned off in notification settings

## 5.64

- Today: a “Same as yesterday” button in the progress line — copies yesterday's checks and “what I took” into today's empty items; the dialog shows exactly what, and you can untick anything
- Also there — “filled automatically: N”: how many items already came from the watch, scale and Health Connect without you

## 5.63

- Program → Charts: a Compare card — any two metrics on one chart (each with its own scale), e.g. sleep and resting heart rate or calories and weight
- Also there — the previous period as a dashed line over the current one and the difference of averages; for two metrics — the strength of their day-by-day link (Pearson r, from 7 days) with a note that a link isn't a cause
- Experiment: the “28 days” chip no longer breaks letter by letter on a narrow screen

## 5.62

- Program → Summary: experiments. Set what you're trying (“bed before 11 pm”), the start, a 7–28 day period and which metrics to watch — Day index, sleep, day rating, steps, water, resting heart rate, weight
- The app compares the experiment period with the same period before it: averages “before” and “during”, the difference and a verdict — clearly better, clearly worse or within normal variation (Welch 95% interval); honestly labelled as not proof of cause

## 5.61

- “What I took” now works like Day checks: each option has its own row in settings — name, section in the tile, “every day” and effect on the Day index; sections show as subheadings and turn off with the same switch
- “Every day” for supplements and vitamins: the tile shows what hasn't been checked yet today, and so does the tile summary
- The Health home is more compact: Today's state, a wide strength tile, and the other sections as a 2 × 3 grid of equal tiles with one number and one summary line

## 5.60

- Health: new Sleep tile and section — last night (times, score, hypnogram, awakenings), regularity, 2 weeks of nights on one map, average stages vs typical
- Sleep Regularity Index (Phillips, 2017): how consistently your sleep falls at the same time of day, 100 — to the minute; compared with UK Biobank (Windred, 2024: median 81, half of people 74–86)
- Also there: bedtime spread, wake time on weekdays and weekends, and social jetlag (weekend shift of mid-sleep)

## 5.55

## 5.51
